About this prototype

A static, dependency-free HTML prototype of the Rubies loyalty flow: earn a ruby with every order from a store, see your progress, and unlock a coupon.

Flow: Home ("Your Rubies and coupons" cards) → Food, coffee → Coffee Lab store (stamp card, tap an item to add it) → Order (payment) → Order status (the stamp card drops in, a new ruby stamp lands with a burst, then the map and pin appear). Each order adds one ruby; after the 4th ruby the demo ends and a Start over button resets it. Use the switch at the top, or tap “Your Rubies and coupons” on Home, to open the Rewards prototype.

Reset: press Cmd + R (Ctrl + R on Windows) to reload and start again from Home.
